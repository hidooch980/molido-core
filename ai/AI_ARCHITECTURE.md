MOLIDO AI ARCHITECTURE


Purpose


The MOLIDO AI layer is the intelligent automation foundation of the MOLIDO ecosystem.


Its purpose is to assist with:




Project management


Content production


Story development


Growth analysis


Community intelligence


Security monitoring


Personalization


Second Brain capabilities


Future autonomous operations





AI Operating Principle


MOLIDO follows:




AI operates. Humans govern.




AI may perform routine, reversible, low-risk tasks automatically.


Sensitive actions require appropriate human authorization.

AUTONOMY LEVELS


Level 0 — Observe


AI only analyzes and reports.


Level 1 — Recommend


AI proposes actions.


Level 2 — Assisted


AI performs actions after approval.


Level 3 — Controlled Automation


AI performs predefined low-risk actions automatically.


Level 4 — Advanced Automation


Only permitted after security, monitoring, rollback, and governance systems are mature.


Level 5 — Autonomous Operations


Not enabled by default.


Requires:




Strong governance


Comprehensive monitoring


Emergency shutdown


Audit logs


Rollback


Security validation


Human governance





EMERGENCY CONTROL


Every autonomous subsystem must eventually support:


STOP
PAUSE
ROLLBACK
DISABLE
AUDIT



The founder must retain the ability to stop sensitive automated operations.



CORE PRINCIPLE


MOLIDO is not designed to replace human governance.


It is designed to amplify human capability through intelligent automation.




AI builds. AI analyzes. AI learns. Humans govern.
