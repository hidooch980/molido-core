# MOLIDO BRAND IDENTITY

### Brand

### MOLIDO

### THE LAST SIGNAL

### Brand Position

MOLIDO is a futuristic global digital universe combining:

AI + Story + Game + Community + Creativity + Security + Future Technology

MOLIDO should feel like a living universe rather than a conventional application.

### BRAND PERSONALITY

MOLIDO is:

- Futuristic
- Intelligent
- Mysterious
- Cinematic
- Global
- Human
- Innovative
- Secure
- Curious
- Optimistic

MOLIDO is not:

- Aggressive
- Deceptive
- Financially manipulative
- Spam-driven
- Fake
- Overpromising

### BRAND PROMISE

MOLIDO creates experiences that make people want to:

Discover.

Play.

Explore.

Create.

Connect.

Return.

### CORE MESSAGE

The signal has arrived.

Supporting message:

The journey begins now.

Story message:

Something is coming. The question is whether you will discover it first.

### VISUAL DIRECTION

The visual identity should communicate:

- Advanced technology
- Mystery
- Global connection
- Artificial intelligence
- Exploration
- Digital discovery

Preferred visual concepts:

- 3D environments
- Global networks
- Light signals
- Digital particles
- Futuristic interfaces
- Abstract symbols
- Cosmic environments
- Digital Earth
- Energy fragments

### COLOR DIRECTION

Primary visual direction:

- Deep dark backgrounds
- High-contrast luminous elements
- Futuristic light effects
- Clean neutral typography

Accent colors may evolve by campaign.

The brand should remain recognizable even when campaign colors change.

### TYPOGRAPHY

Typography should be:

- Modern
- Clean
- Highly readable
- Suitable for mobile
- Suitable for cinematic titles
- Suitable for multilingual content

The exact production font stack will be defined during website implementation.

### LOGO

The MOLIDO logo should be:

- Simple
- Recognizable
- Scalable
- Suitable for mobile icons
- Suitable for website headers
- Suitable for video end cards
- Suitable for social media profiles

The logo must work in:

- Light background
- Dark background
- Monochrome
- Small-size applications

### THE LAST SIGNAL SYMBOL

The future visual language may use a distinctive signal symbol representing:

Discovery + Connection + Unknown + Future

The symbol should be original and not imitate existing brands.

### VIDEO STYLE

Default video direction:

- Cinematic
- Fast but understandable
- Strong visual transitions
- Atmospheric sound
- Clear subtitles
- Mobile-first composition

Avoid excessive visual complexity that makes the story difficult to understand.

### POSTER STYLE

Posters should:

- Have one clear focal point
- Use strong visual hierarchy
- Preserve negative space
- Use readable typography
- Communicate one idea
- Maintain MOLIDO identity

Default title:

MOLIDO

Secondary:

### THE LAST SIGNAL

### SOCIAL MEDIA STYLE

Posts should be:

- Short
- Intriguing
- Easy to understand
- Visually consistent
- Shareable
- Honest

Use curiosity rather than deception.

### LANGUAGE

MOLIDO is a global brand.

The master language for the core brand should be English.

Localized content may support:

- Persian
- Arabic
- Hindi
- Urdu
- Chinese
- Turkish
- Azerbaijani
- Additional languages

Translations should preserve meaning rather than simply translating words literally.

### COUNTRY EXPERIENCE

Country selection may influence:

- Language
- Content
- Publishing time
- Community
- Local campaigns
- Regional challenges

Country localization must not create discriminatory access to basic safety or privacy rights.

```text
AI BRAND CONTROL

 ↓

 ↓

```

### COPYRIGHT

MOLIDO must not intentionally copy:

- Existing logos
- Famous characters
- Protected fictional universes
- Music without permission
- Images without appropriate rights
- Other companies' branding

AI-generated assets should be reviewed for potential intellectual-property concerns before commercial use.

### BRAND TRUST

MOLIDO must protect long-term trust.

Never use:

- Fake testimonials
- Fake user counts
- Fake partnerships
- Fake celebrity endorsements
- Fake revenue claims
- Fake scarcity
- Fake giveaways

If something is planned, say it is planned.

If something exists, show evidence.

### COMMUNICATION RULE

MOLIDO communicates with confidence without pretending to know what it does not know.

The brand should say:

"Coming soon"

when something is not yet available.

It should never present future functionality as an existing product.
